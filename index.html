What is the cost and what does it include? +


The fee is $499 USD for one participant, or $798 USD for two. This includes full admission, materials, and lunch daily. Scholarships are available.

June 8th - 11th. Official hours are 8:30 AM to 4:30 PM. Drop-off begins at 8:00 AM, and pick-up is by 4:45 PM.

Discovery Children's Museum (Discovery Hall) near downtown Las Vegas. Parking is free in their structure.

What is the refund policy? +


100% refund if canceled 90+ days prior. 50% refund 31-89 days prior. No refunds within 30 days of the event.

Phones are welcome and encouraged for research! However, they must be stowed during certain lessons to ensure full engagement.
